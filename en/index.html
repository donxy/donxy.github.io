Method

Figures only, not methods

Each issue looks only at the first three figures of one paper: get the originals, zoom in and crop, then talk design. Measured color values and dimensions are labelled as measured; design intent is labelled as inferred — guesses never pose as facts, the same discipline as the deep-read notes.
